Connects your WordPress site to Site Guardian for health checks and AI-assisted maintenance.

== Description ==

Site Guardian connects your site to the Site Guardian service, which checks your site's health and answers maintenance questions with AI assistance. Your site proves ownership of its domain during registration, and its credential is stored encrypted.
